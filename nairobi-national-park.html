Nairobi National Park Safari

Experience the unique wildlife of Nairobi National Park, one of the world's few national parks located next to a capital city. Enjoy a guided game drive with Stema Trans and discover wildlife, scenery and the story of Nairobi's famous conservation area.

Duration 4–5 Hours
Group 1–7 People
Departure Nairobi
Experience Game Drive

About the Experience

Our Nairobi National Park experience is ideal for visitors who want to experience Kenya's wildlife without travelling far from Nairobi. The park offers opportunities to see lions, giraffes, zebras, buffaloes, rhinos, antelopes and many species of birds.

Your Stema Trans driver-guide will accompany you throughout the experience, helping you understand the wildlife, landscape, conservation and history of the park.

Suggested Itinerary

  • Pickup: Pickup from your Nairobi hotel, residence or agreed location.
  • Journey to the park: Drive to Nairobi National Park and proceed through the park entrance.
  • Game drive: Explore the park's different habitats while looking for wildlife.
  • Wildlife viewing: Possible sightings include lions, rhinos, giraffes, zebras, buffaloes, antelopes and numerous bird species.
  • Refreshment break: Optional stop depending on the agreed itinerary and park conditions.
  • Return: Exit the park and drive back to your Nairobi drop-off location.
