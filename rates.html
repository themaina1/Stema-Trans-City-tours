Professional Nairobi transport, tours and guided experiences at transparent and competitive rates.

How Our Rates Work

Vehicle-based tours and transfers are charged per vehicle, not per passenger.

For example, a Tour Van costing KES 5,500 / approximately USD 42 can carry the agreed passenger capacity without charging KES 5,500 for every individual passenger.

City Walks are charged per person because they are walking experiences rather than vehicle hire.

Park entrance fees and attraction admission fees are separate unless specifically stated as included.

What's Included & Excluded

Included

  • Agreed vehicle
  • Professional driver / driver-guide
  • Fuel for the agreed itinerary
  • Pickup within the agreed service area
  • Transport during the agreed tour
  • Basic assistance during the journey

Not Included

  • National park entrance fees
  • Attraction entrance fees
  • Meals and drinks
  • Accommodation
  • Special activities
  • Personal expenses
  • Additional parking charges where applicable
  • Extra mileage or hours outside the agreed itinerary

Important Booking Notes

1. Vehicle rates: Unless otherwise stated, vehicle-based rates are charged per vehicle, not per passenger.

2. Passenger capacity: The number of passengers must remain within the safe/legal capacity of the selected vehicle.

3. Attraction fees: Park and attraction entrance fees are separate unless specifically stated in the quotation.

4. Extra time: Additional hours beyond the agreed itinerary may attract an additional charge.

5. Out-of-town travel: Long-distance journeys are quoted according to the complete itinerary.

6. USD prices: USD figures are approximate conversions for international clients. Final payment may be made in Kenyan Shillings according to the agreed quotation.

7. Rates: Displayed rates are starting rates and may vary according to date, itinerary, vehicle availability, distance and special requirements.
