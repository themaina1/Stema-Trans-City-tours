Nairobi National Park Safari

Enjoy a guided game drive through Nairobi National Park, one of the world's most unique wildlife destinations, located just outside Nairobi. Visitors may encounter lions, giraffes, zebras, buffaloes, rhinos, antelopes, warthogs and many species of birds depending on the season and location within the park.

Duration: 4–5 Hours
Group: 1–7 People
Departure: Nairobi
Experience: Guided Game Drive
Vehicle: Tour Van or Land Cruiser

Transport Package
