About Nairobi National Park

Nairobi National Park is one of Kenya's most unique wildlife destinations. Located just outside Nairobi, the park offers visitors the opportunity to experience wildlife while still being close to the city.

A typical game drive can include sightings of zebras, giraffes, buffaloes, antelopes, lions, rhinos and many species of birds, depending on season, time of day and wildlife movement.

Stema Trans provides private vehicle transport and driver-guide services for guests who want to explore the park comfortably from Nairobi.
