Nairobi National Park Experience

Nairobi National Park offers a unique opportunity to experience Kenya's wildlife only a short distance from the city centre.

Guests can enjoy a guided game drive through open grasslands and wildlife habitats while looking for animals such as lions, giraffes, zebras, buffaloes, rhinos, antelopes and many bird species.

Stema Trans provides comfortable transport and driver-guide services for guests visiting the park from Nairobi.

What You Can Experience

Wildlife Viewing

Search for lions, rhinos, giraffes, zebras, buffaloes, antelopes and other wildlife.

Bird Watching

The park supports a wide variety of birdlife, including resident and migratory species.

Scenic Landscapes

Enjoy open grasslands, seasonal wetlands, acacia landscapes and views towards Nairobi.

Private Game Drive

Travel with your own group and enjoy a flexible experience with a Stema Trans driver-guide.
