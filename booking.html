Nairobi National Park Safari

Experience the unique wildlife of Nairobi National Park, one of the world's few national parks located next to a capital city.

Discover Nairobi National Park

Enjoy a guided game drive through Nairobi National Park with Stema Trans and discover wildlife, beautiful scenery and the fascinating conservation story of Nairobi's famous national park.

The park offers the unusual experience of seeing wildlife against the backdrop of Nairobi's skyline. Depending on the season and conditions, guests may encounter lions, giraffes, zebras, buffaloes, rhinos, elands, warthogs, hippos and many species of birds.
