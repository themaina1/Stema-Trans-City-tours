Explore Nairobi on Foot

Experience Nairobi beyond the vehicle. Our guided city walks introduce visitors to the history, culture, architecture, markets and everyday life of Kenya's capital.

City walks are ideal for visitors who have a few hours in Nairobi and want to experience the city with a knowledgeable local guide.

  • Local Food & Cultural Experience
